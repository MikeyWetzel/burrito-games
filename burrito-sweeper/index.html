How to play

  • Double tap a tile to reveal. First reveal is always safe.
  • Single tap a hidden tile to mark it. Tapping a marked tile does nothing.
  • Double tap a marked tile to unmark it; double tap again to reveal.
  • The mark icon is automatic: a nacho chip means 0–1 burritos hide next to it, an avocado 2–3, a burrito 4 or more. The counter counts every mark.
  • Drag to pan · pinch (or wheel) to zoom · Fit board recenters.
  • Double tap a number with the right burrito count to chord. Boards are logic-solvable — no forced guesses.
